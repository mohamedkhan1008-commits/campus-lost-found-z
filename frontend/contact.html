Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a lost item?

First, sign up for an account if you haven't already. Then, go to "Report Lost Item" and fill in the details about your lost item including title, category, location, and description. You can also add an image for better identification.

How do I report a found item?

Sign up for an account, then click "Report Found Item". Fill in the details about the item you found. The owner can then contact you through the platform email.

Can I delete my posted item?

Yes! Once you log in to your dashboard, you can delete any item you've posted. You can also mark items as "resolved" when they're found or returned.

How do I contact someone about their item?

On each item listing, you'll find a "Contact Poster" button with their email address. You can email them directly to inquire about the item or if you found their lost item.

Is my personal information safe?

Yes, your information is kept private and secure. Your email is only visible to other users when they try to contact you about your posted items.

How long are items displayed?

Items remain active on the platform indefinitely until you manually delete them or mark them as resolved. We recommend updating your items periodically.

What if I found the owner of an item I found?

Contact the person who posted the found item directly using the email link provided. Arrange a meeting on campus to return the item.

Is this service free?

Yes! Campus Lost & Found is completely free to use. It's a community service to help students recover their lost belongings.
